Architecture

Build the applications your business actually needs.

Imagine a wholesale business using three different applications. All three are built on Alfabase, so all three share the same underlying business foundation.

01Three applications

The warehouse application

A simple interface built for receiving stock, scanning products and recording inventory movements. No unnecessary menus. No accounting screens. Just the tools warehouse employees need.

The sales application

A separate application designed for the sales team to create orders, check inventory and issue invoices. It works with the same product and inventory records as the warehouse application.

The accounting agent

An AI agent that processes financial transactions, reconciles bank activity and helps prepare financial reports. It does not need to navigate either application’s interface. It works directly with the underlying ERP engine.

02Three applications. One set of records.

Because all three are built on Alfabase, they share the same underlying business foundation. A change to a business record does not have to be separately recreated in every application.

And when the business wants to replace its warehouse application, it can build a new one against the same engine without migrating the entire ERP. Change the applications. Keep the business system.
