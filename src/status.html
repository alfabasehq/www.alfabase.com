Development status

We are building the foundation first.

Alfabase is currently under active development and is not yet available to customers. Nothing on this site is something you can use today.

01Why the foundation comes first

Our first priority is building a complete, reliable ERP engine and the shared model that applications can depend on. We are taking this approach because compatibility requires a foundation that developers can trust.

A partial foundation is not a foundation. Every gap is a place where a developer has to invent their own model again, and that is the one outcome a shared engine exists to prevent.

The ambition is simple. Make Alfabase the common ERP engine for the next generation of business software.

02A new engine, not a first attempt

Alfabase is being built by the developer of Manager.io, accounting software used by more than a million businesses. It already covers much of the ground a complete ERP foundation has to cover.

The engine itself is new. It is being written from scratch rather than extracted from Manager.io, with a data model informed by that experience. That is why the scope is broad from the start: we know what the foundation has to hold before applications can rely on it.

03Where things stand

The engine is in active development. There is no public API, no SDK and no sign-up yet. When there is something developers can build against, this page is where it will be announced first.
