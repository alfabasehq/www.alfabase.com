Planned modules

The ERP underneath everything.

A shared ERP foundation is only useful if it can represent the business activities applications depend on. That is why we are building Alfabase around a comprehensive ERP model, rather than a minimal ledger that leaves every developer to fill in the gaps.

01Forty-two modules across business operations

The planned foundation spans six areas. A standard with gaps is not a standard: each gap is a place where a developer invents their own model again, which is the one outcome a shared engine exists to prevent. So forty-two modules is the scope the first release is being built to, not a subset we intend to grow from.

None of it is finished. Every module below is intended scope, not a delivered feature. Nothing on this list is something you can use today, and we will say which parts are built and verified when there are any.

Sales and receivables

  • Estimates and quotes
  • Sales orders
  • Billing and invoicing
  • Recurring billing
  • Customisable invoices
  • Accounts receivable
  • Aged receivables

Purchasing and payables

  • Purchase orders
  • Accounts payable
  • Aged payables
  • Remittance advices
  • Expense claims
  • Billable expenses
  • Credit notes

Inventory, assets and operations

  • Stock and inventory
  • Manufacturing management
  • Delivery notes
  • Fixed asset management
  • Capital accounts
  • Payroll management
  • Time and service billing

Cash and accounting

  • Cash management
  • Bank reconciliation
  • Bank statement importing
  • General ledger
  • Chart of accounts
  • Journal entries
  • Multi-currency

Business structure and rules

  • VAT, GST or sales tax
  • Cash-basis accounting
  • Accrual-basis accounting
  • Project-based accounting
  • Departmental accounting
  • Custom fields
  • Email templates

Financial reporting

  • Profit and loss statement
  • Balance sheet
  • Statement of changes in equity
  • Trial balance
  • Comparative reporting
  • Drill-down reports
  • Customer statements

One consistent foundation across business operations.
